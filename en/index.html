What is TakeAny.AI? TakeAny.AI is a sovereign Control Plane and AI agent orchestration platform built for On-Premise and Air-Gap deployments. It runs as a single Go binary on a local SQLite database, providing a hardware-isolated Firecracker MicroVM runtime and no fees for execution steps (0% Step Tax).

How is TakeAny.AI different from n8n? Unlike n8n, TakeAny.AI is designed strictly for autonomous AI agents. Instead of charging for every decision step in the loop (step tax), it bills for the completed task as a whole, with MicroVM hardware code isolation and full EU AI Act and GDPR coverage.
