01 · Why RAG

Answers for your people right away, without reading hundreds of pages

Manuals, guidelines, policies and contracts become an assistant that answers a question in seconds and shows which document the answer came from. You run the system directly on a company server or a workstation — no files sent to an external cloud, no charges per decision step, and full source verification.

Fewer repeat questions for your experts

The assistant handles the routine questions, so support staff and consultants spend their time on the cases that genuinely need a person.

Support at every stage of a project

The assistant helps with planning and requirement checks (state aid rules, eligibility criteria) as well as day-to-day procedural questions during delivery.

Answers that point back to the source

The assistant draws only on your organisation’s documents and names the chapter, page and clause, so every answer can be checked at the source.

Available outside working hours

Employees, beneficiaries and clients get help in the evening and at the weekend, without adding headcount.

What changes in practice

Finding information drops from half an hour of paging through PDFs to a short conversation. Confidential guidelines and documents stay on your own infrastructure and are never used to train public AI models (GDPR, EU AI Act).

A complete toolkit to launch and run RAG inside your company

TakeAny.AI is not a connector to someone else's RAG service. It is the full toolkit: you configure the knowledge base and how it is indexed, watch search and indexing as they run, and tune the chunking and inference prompts — all from one console, on your own infrastructure.

Full isolation, GDPR and EU AI Act compliance

Commercial documents, contracts, medical and financial data never leave your infrastructure. Operations are protected by hardware-level Firecracker microVM isolation and dual safety firewalls.

03 ·How it works

Turn an outdated knowledge base into a secure AI assistant: a four-step conversion

01

Define the base

Name and describe the repository, then bind the LLM provider and the chunking model that will process it.

02

Index on isolated compute

The RAG Indexer helper VM parses and indexes documents away from your main systems. Add the Jina Reader helper when a source is a URL rather than a file.

03

Upload, then index the full base

Raw files sit unindexed until you run indexing. A progress bar tracks raw, processed and doc counts until the helper powers off.

04

Query with grounded answers

The agent searches, reads the top documents, traverses related chunks, and answers from retrieved facts — citing pages, or saying the evidence is absent.

04 ·Why it is secure

Sovereignty is structural here

Four properties of the design, not four settings you have to remember to switch on.

Every answer is checkable against a source.

Strict JSON output and page-level source tracking give full traceability. Every answer is verifiable against an internal source document, which is what prevents a confident invention.

No third party retains your documents.

Indexes live in host-managed environments, so third-party providers cannot retain proprietary enterprise data or train on it.

Missing evidence is stated, not filled in.

Instructing the model to state missing evidence enforces a zero-trust boundary: no unauthorised assumptions, and no leakage across domain scopes.

Processing never touches shared memory.

Processing and indexing run on dedicated, isolated virtual machines, keeping raw documents and indexing tasks away from external network vectors and shared memory spaces.
